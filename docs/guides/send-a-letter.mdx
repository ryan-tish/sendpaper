Letters suit anything that needs more than a postcard: a note to a landlord, a reply to a notice, a thank-you or condolence letter, or a letter to your representative.

> Write a short letter to my landlord at 77 Elm St, Unit 2, Portland OR 97205, saying I'm renewing the lease for another year, and mail it. From me at the same address, apartment 4B.

## What happens

- The agent drafts the text and reads it back to you.
- It calls `create_letter` with the body (plain text; blank lines become paragraphs) and `font` set to `serif` or `sans`.
- The letter prints in black and white on 8.5 × 11 paper, up to about 3 pages, with the addresses showing through the envelope window.

## Certified Mail

When you need proof, send it by **USPS Certified Mail**:

| Option | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular letter | $4.99 | USPS First-Class |
| Certified | $14.99 | A USPS tracking number and proof of mailing and delivery |
| Certified + return receipt | $19.99 | Everything above, plus the recipient's signature as proof of delivery |

Ask your agent to "send it certified" (or "with a return receipt"). It calls `create_letter` with `certified` set to `certified` or `certified_return_receipt`. Once USPS accepts the letter, the tracking number appears on the order page and in `get_order` as `tracking.number`, with a USPS tracking link.

<Tip>Leases, courts and agencies often ask for a return receipt. If you're not sure, choose Certified + return receipt.</Tip>
