---
title: "What is Sendpaper?"
description: "Physical mail for AI agents. Ask your agent to send a postcard or letter, approve it, and Sendpaper prints and mails it."
---

Sendpaper lets an AI agent send **real postcards and letters** to US addresses. You ask in a sentence: *"mail my mom a birthday postcard"*. Your agent writes it, shows you exactly what will print, pays with your approval, and Sendpaper prints it and hands it to USPS First-Class, usually within one business day.

It works with **Codex, Muse, Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code** and anything that speaks [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) or plain HTTP.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect once">Add the Sendpaper MCP server to your agent. It takes one command and no sign-up. [Quickstart →](/quickstart)</Step>
  <Step title="Ask">"Send a thank-you postcard to…" Your agent confirms the address and wording with you.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve and pay">Your agent shows the exact print preview. It can [pay for you with a one-time Stripe token](/guides/agent-payments) you approve, or you pay a regular checkout link.</Step>
  <Step title="We mail it">A person reviews every piece, prints it and mails it via USPS First-Class. Delivery usually takes 3–5 business days.</Step>
</Steps>
