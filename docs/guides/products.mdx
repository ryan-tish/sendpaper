## Postcards

| | 4 × 6 | 6 × 9 | 6 × 11 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $2.99 | $3.99 | $5.99 |
| Message | up to 600 characters | up to 600 characters | up to 600 characters |
| Front image | at least 1800 × 1200 px recommended | at least 2700 × 1800 px recommended | at least 3300 × 1800 px recommended |

- **Front layouts:** a big headline on a solid color (`ink`, `sky`, `sunset`, `forest`, `rose`, `sand`, `night`, `mint`) in serif, sans or script; one photo cropped to fill the card; a photo with a caption band; or a collage of 2 to 4 photos.
- **Back:** the message on the left in handwriting, serif or sans; your return address, the recipient and postage on the right.
- **Express (+$10.00):** USPS Priority Mail, usually 2–3 days, with tracking.

## Letters

- $4.99 for up to about 3 single-spaced pages (9,000 characters) on 8.5 × 11 paper, folded into a #10 window envelope.
- **Photo (optional, +$1.00):** one image printed under the date, up to 6.5 × 3 in. A letter with a photo prints in color; without one it prints in black and white.
- The return and recipient addresses print at the top of the first page so they show through the window.
- The text is plain text. Blank lines become paragraph breaks.
- **Your own PDF:** upload a PDF (or give your agent a link to one) of up to 6 pages instead of typing the letter. Any page size is fitted to 8.5 × 11, and a blank address page is added in front, so your document needs no room for addresses. Black and white, or color for $1.00 more.
- **Express (+$10.00):** USPS Priority Mail, usually 2–3 days, with tracking. Not available with Certified Mail, which already includes tracking.
- **Certified Mail:** $14.99 with a USPS tracking number and proof of mailing and delivery, or $19.99 with a return receipt (the recipient's signature). See [Send a letter](/guides/send-a-letter#certified-mail).

## Delivery

US addresses only (including PR, US territories and APO/FPO). Mailed via USPS First-Class, usually 3–5 business days after mailing. Certified letters travel the same way, with USPS tracking.
