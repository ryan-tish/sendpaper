# Taxes & government

## Reply to an IRS or state tax notice

Notices give a deadline and an address. A certified reply proves when you answered, and you can mail the documents they ask for as a PDF.

```text
I got IRS notice CP2000 about income I already reported. Draft a short response citing the notice number and explaining it's on my Schedule C, and mail it certified with a return receipt to the address on the notice.
```

*Certified letter + return receipt*. Use the address on your notice. Including documents? Combine them with your letter into one PDF.

## Writing to your representatives

Offices count physical letters. A short, personal one is read.

```text
Draft a one-page letter to my state senator supporting the library funding bill, in my words, and mail it to their district office.
```

*Letter*

## Request records from an agency

Records requests often go by mail, and the response clock starts when they receive it. A certified letter shows the date.

```text
Draft a public records request to the city clerk at 200 Center St, Springfield IL 62701, asking for the 2025 building permits on my street, and mail it certified.
```

*Certified letter*

# Housing

## Notice to your landlord

Leases usually require written notice for move-outs, renewals and repairs, and a dated delivery record settles arguments later.

```text
Send my landlord this 30-day notice that I'm not renewing the lease, by certified mail with a return receipt. Return address is my apartment.
```

*Certified letter + return receipt*. The return receipt adds the landlord's signature.

## Ask for your security deposit back

If your deposit hasn't come back, a clear written demand with proof it arrived is usually the next step.

```text
Write a polite but firm letter to my former landlord asking for my $1,800 security deposit back, listing my move-out date and forwarding address, and send it certified with a return receipt.
```

*Certified letter + return receipt*. Rules and deadlines for deposits vary by state; check yours before you send.

## Moving and address changes

Tell the people who still send you paper where to find you.

```text
Send a 'We moved!' postcard with our new address to everyone on this list.
```

*Postcard 4×6*. One order per recipient.

# Money & credit

## Dispute an error on your credit report

You can dispute errors with the credit bureaus by mail, and a certified letter with copies of your evidence leaves a clear record.

```text
Draft a dispute letter to the credit bureau about the late payment on my report that was actually paid on time, and mail it certified to the dispute address on the bureau's website.
```

*Certified letter*. Use the bureau's dispute address. Including evidence? Combine it with your letter into one PDF.

## Ask a collector to validate a debt

If a collector contacts you about a debt you don't recognize, asking in writing for proof of the debt, with a delivery record, keeps things documented.

```text
Write a debt validation request to this collection agency about account 44129, asking them to verify the debt and the amount, and send it certified with a return receipt.
```

*Certified letter + return receipt*

## Chase an unpaid invoice

When emails go unanswered, a formal demand letter with proof of delivery often gets an invoice paid, and it's the record you'll need if it escalates.

```text
Draft a formal payment demand to Acme Studio for invoice #1042 ($3,200, 60 days overdue), giving them 14 days to pay, and mail it certified.
```

*Certified letter*

# Contracts & documents

## Cancel a contract or membership

Some contracts and memberships only accept cancellation in writing. A certified letter proves you sent it and when.

```text
Write a letter cancelling my gym membership (member ID 88213) effective at the end of this month, and send it certified with a return receipt to their head office.
```

*Certified letter + return receipt*

## Mail a signed form or document

Signed forms, applications and statements still go by mail. Upload the PDF and it's printed and mailed, with Certified Mail if you need proof.

```text
Mail this signed PDF to the county clerk at 100 Main St, Springfield IL 62701, by certified mail.
```

*Certified letter*. Up to 6 pages; any page size is fitted to 8.5×11, and an address page is added in front.

## Send a formal complaint to a company

When chat and email go nowhere, a dated letter to the company's address puts the complaint on record.

```text
Write a firm, polite complaint to Acme Appliances, 50 Market St, Columbus OH 43215, about the dishwasher that failed twice under warranty, asking for a replacement, and mail it certified.
```

*Certified letter*

# Celebrations

## Birthdays and holidays

A card on the fridge beats a text, and your agent can write it in a minute.

```text
Send Mom a 6×9 birthday postcard with a collage of these photos of the kids and a short note from me.
```

*Postcard 6×9*

## Thank-you notes

After an interview, a favor, or a job well done, a handwritten-style note gets remembered.

```text
Send a thank-you postcard to the contractor who fixed our roof. Their office is 500 Main St, Boston MA 02110.
```

*Postcard 4×6*

## Weddings, babies and big news

Announcements are the mail people stick on the fridge. Add a photo and send one to each person on your list.

```text
Make a 6×9 postcard with this photo of the baby, the caption "Hello, world: Maya, born June 2", and mail one to my grandmother at 8 Elm St, Dayton OH 45402.
```

*Postcard 6×9*. One order per recipient.

# Life & community

## Small business

A postcard to a first-time customer or a lapsed regular stands out in a way email can't.

```text
Send a thank-you postcard with our logo to each of this week's new customers from the Shopify export.
```

*Postcard 4×6*. One order per recipient, and only to people who bought from you. No bulk marketing.

## Condolences and get-well wishes

Some moments deserve paper. Your agent can help with the words, but the card is real.

```text
Help me write a short, warm condolence note to Aunt Ruth and mail it as a letter. Keep it simple.
```

*Letter*

## Staying in touch with family

Grandparents and relatives who aren't online still check the mailbox. A regular letter keeps them in the loop.

```text
Turn these notes about our month into a one-page letter to Grandpa Joe at 31 Lake Rd, Duluth MN 55802, in a warm, newsy tone, and mail it.
```

*Letter*
