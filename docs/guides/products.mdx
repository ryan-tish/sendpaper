<Tip>**First postcard free:** any size, one per return address. It's applied automatically when the order is created; confirm it on the order page with no card needed.</Tip>

## Postcards

| | 4 × 6 | 6 × 9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $2.99 | $3.99 |
| Message | up to 600 characters | up to 600 characters |
| Front image | at least 1800 × 1200 px recommended | at least 2700 × 1800 px recommended |

- **Front:** your photo, cropped to fill the card, or a big headline on a solid color (`ink`, `sky`, `sunset`, `forest`).
- **Back:** the message on the left in a handwriting-style font; your return address, the recipient and postage on the right.

## Letters

- $4.99 for up to about 3 single-spaced pages (9,000 characters) on 8.5 × 11 paper, black and white, folded into a #10 window envelope.
- The return and recipient addresses print at the top of the first page so they show through the window.
- Plain text only. Blank lines become paragraph breaks.

## Delivery

US addresses only (including PR, US territories and APO/FPO). Mailed via USPS First-Class, usually 3–5 business days after mailing.
